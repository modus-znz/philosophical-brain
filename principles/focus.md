# Focus & Discipline

> "Concentrate all your thoughts upon the work in hand. The sun's rays do not burn until brought to a focus." — [[Alexander Graham Bell]]
> "Discipline is the bridge between goals and accomplishment." — [[Jim Rohn]]

**Definition:** Direct sustained attention at a single target. Mastery of
self precedes mastery of work — no external tooling substitutes for deliberate,
undistracted effort.

## Core Axiom

Depth beats breadth. A system built one focused session at a time outlasts a
system sketched across fragmented attention. Discipline is not a constraint but
a liberation: it is the bridge from intention to outcome.

## Code Directives

- Work in bounded, single-purpose sessions; one change set at a time.
- Order tasks by dependency; finish what blocks everything else first.
- Silence noise — notifications, tab sprawl, context switches — before deep work.
- Make the goal concrete ("ship the refund endpoint") before starting.
