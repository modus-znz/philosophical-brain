# Patience

> "Nature does not hurry, yet everything is accomplished." — [[Lao Tzu]]
> "Pole pole ndiyo mwendo." (Slowly, slowly is indeed the way.) — Swahili proverb

**Definition:** Endure the slow, incremental path without desperation. Trust
that correct, steady work compounds. Patience is not passivity — it is the
discipline to not force an outcome before its conditions are met.

## Core Axiom

Every wound heals by degrees; every hard problem yields to sustained, unhurried
pressure. The engineer who can hold attention on a stubborn defect longer than
the urge to thrash wins.

## Code Directives

- Stay with an intermittent bug; gather data over several reproductions before changing hypotheses.
- Let builds, deployments, and slow tests finish — don't thrash on parallel work.
- When blocked, step back and let understanding arrive rather than forcing code.
- Trust the compounding of small daily improvements over impatient shortcuts.
