# Kaizen — Continuous Improvement

> "Success is the sum of small efforts, repeated day in and day out." — [[Robert Collier]]
> "Drops of water hollow out the stone, not by force but by falling often." — [[Ovid]]

**Definition:** Japanese philosophy of *continuous improvement* — small,
incremental, compounding changes rather than dramatic overhauls. In
production engineering: optimize the workflow, not just the output; make the
process itself steadily better.

## Core Axiom

Perfection is not a destination but a direction. The compounding effect of many
small, well-targeted improvements outpaces any single heroic refactor. Improve
the *system* of production (tooling, feedback loops, handoffs, automation) and
the product improves as a byproduct.

## Code Directives

- Ship small, safe increments; never hold work hostage to a perfect release.
- Establish a fast, short feedback loop (test → deploy → observe) and shorten it constantly.
- Instrument the production pipeline; make each step visible so it can improve.
- Automate the repetitive; eliminate the wasteful loop before optimizing the product.
- Refactor continuously in small slices rather than in big-bang rewrites.
