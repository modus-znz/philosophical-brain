## Heritage

Zen (Chan) is Buddhism distilled: no doctrine, just the unmuddied mind present
with what is. Its practice — sitting, breath, the koan — is attention as
discipline, and its paradox is that the answer and the question are the same
moment.

## Code Directive

Do the daily work as if it were the whole practice — there is no moment after
enlightenment that excuses the one now. Presence is the discipline: name the
thing blocking the work and it becomes the way through.
