## Heritage

France is the homeland of clear reason and the sharpened sentence. From
Montaigne's essays (try everything, doubt everything) through the *clarté* of
the Enlightenment — Descartes, Voltaire, the *encyclopédistes* — to the modern
craft of Proust and Camus, French wisdom prizes precision of expression and the
courage to question authority with a polite smile.

## Code Directive

Say exactly what the code does, in the fewest true words — French *clarté* is
the engineering discipline of naming. Doubt what compiles; prove what claims to
work; and refine. The correctly named thing needs no comment, and the
well-made small thing outlives the sprawling big one.
