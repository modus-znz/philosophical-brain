## Heritage

Taoism is the art of flowing with the river instead of against it. The *Tao Te
Ching* (Lao Tzu) and the *Zhuangzi* preach wu-wei — action without strain —
and the wisdom of yielding, emptiness, and the uncarved block. Fewer, not
more.

## Code Directive

Remove before you add. The system that is least forced is the one that lasts —
lean on the natural grain of the architecture, cut the parts that fight it, and
stop while there is still room. Effort, applied wrongly, is just friction.
