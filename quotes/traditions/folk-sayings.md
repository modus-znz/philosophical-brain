## Heritage

Every people's anonymous wisdom — the proverb — is a compressed engineering
manual for life: a warning, a plan, a law of nature tested by generations.
Across continents the same truths resurface in different dress. This file is
the resource library: folk sayings from the world over, kept when they change
the way you build.
