## Heritage

Chinese philosophy spans Confucian cultivation, Daoist flow, and the strategy
of the hundred schools. The classics (`Analects`, *Tao Te Ching*, *Art of
War*) root discipline in self-cultivation rather than reward.

## Lineage

- **Confucius** — the superior man: ritual, learning, filial duty ([[learning]] · [[preparation]] · [[progress]] · [[action]] · [[humility-and-learning]])
- **Lao Tzu** — the sage-ruler: effortless action, simplicity, knowing when to stop ([[taoism]])
- **Sun Tzu** — the strategist: win before the battle begins ([[preparation]] · [[security]])
- **Zhuangzi** — the butterfly: ease, transformation, embrace of paradox
- **Xunzi** — the teacher: learning is earned, goodness is cultivated

## Code Directive

Improve by small deliberate strokes, never by heroic force. Know yourself
before you act, and let ritual become habit: the mountain is moved by carrying
stones, the road by single steps, the self by daily renewal.
