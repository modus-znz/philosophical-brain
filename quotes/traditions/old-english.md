## Heritage

The Anglo-Saxon mind is a northern mind — short of sugar, long on endurance.
Its wisdom lives in gnomic maxims, proverbs, and the great epics (*Beowulf*,
the Exeter Book riddles) that treat fate, loyalty, and the "good name" as the
only things that survive a man. Old English is the root dialect of the English
that engineering now speaks.

## Code Directive

Do the deed, then speak the deed. The Anglo-Saxons measured a man by his
actions and his word; code is the same bargain — commit what you can stand
behind, and let the work, not the talk, carry your name. Endurance beats
brilliance on any long road.
