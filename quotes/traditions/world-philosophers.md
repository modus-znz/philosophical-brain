## Heritage

Philosophers are the engineers of meaning — they build, test, and refactor the
ideas a civilization runs on. Across millennia and continents they converge on
the same load-bearing principles: know yourself, question your tools, prefer
the careful path, and let the work outlast you.

## Code Directive

Before building, ask the universal question every thinker of every tradition
has asked: *what is being assumed?* Then act on the answer. Tools, frameworks,
and fashions differ by century; the discipline of the examined work does not.
