## Heritage

From the golden age of Athens to the closing Rome, the classical mind gave the
West its founding categories: reason, virtue, rhetoric, and the examined life.
Socrates questioned, Plato systematized, Aristotle measured, and the Stoics
turned philosophy into a practice for ordinary days.

## Code Directive

Reason before reply; facts before feeling; examine the assumptions underneath.
The classical method is the first-principles method: entertain the idea without
accepting it, argue the good rather than the convenient, and treat each day's
build as a finite, countable life.
