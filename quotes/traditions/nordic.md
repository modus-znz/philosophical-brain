## Heritage

The Norse view is stark and honest: the world is a long winter, and what
survives it is what you do — the deed, the name, the faithful oath. The
*Hávamál* ("Sayings of the High One") is the north's own wisdom book: pithy,
practical, unsentimental.

## Lineage

- **The Hávamál** — Odin's counsel: deeds outlive the man ([[progress]] · [[learning]])

## Code Directive

Ship the deed, not the talk. A reputation — in code, in commits, in support but
the same rule — is the only asset that outlives you: build so that the work can
be believed after you are gone. Careful heads keep their knowledge, and do not
flood the channel with noise.
