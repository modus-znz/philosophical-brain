> "Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu." (A person is a person through other persons.) — Nguni Bantu proverb, the root of Ubuntu philosophy

> "I am because we are." — Ubuntu philosophy, common rendering

> "Harambee" (let us all pull together) — Kenyan/East African communal-effort principle

> "Utu ni jamii." (Humanity is community.) — Swahili phrasing composed for this vault, reflecting Ubuntu thought; not an attested proverb

> "A person is a person because of other people." — [[Desmond Tutu]], on Ubuntu

> "My humanity is bound up in yours, for we can only be human together." — [[Desmond Tutu]]

> "One who does not travel widely thinks his mother is the only cook." — Nigerian (Igbo) proverb, on the value of community and shared knowledge

> "The child who is not embraced by the village will burn it down to feel its warmth." — attributed to an African (often cited as Igbo/Cameroonian) proverb, popularized by poet [[Ijeoma Umebinyuo]]

## Code Directive

The project is a village; its members are persons-through-others. Shared
context, code review, and lifting others are not courtesy — they are how the
whole system stays trustworthy.
