## Code Directive

Treat the unknown as a map to be filled, not a wall to be feared. Discovery is
re-framing the familiar through new eyes — spend effort asking the right
question before hunting the answer. Curiosity is the engine; wandering is the
method; documentation is the new landscape you leave behind.
